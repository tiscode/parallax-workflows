How it runs

One monthly publishing cycle.

Your cadence, unchanged. Parallax fits between publish and deliver: one-way, draft until approved.

01PublishYour CIO publishes the house view on your existing cadence.
→
02IngestParallax pulls the view through the connector and aligns bottom-up data against it.
→
03StructureImplementable names surface under each tilt with source provenance. Draft-level.
→
04ApproveYour named owner reviews and approves at the human overlay.
→
05DeliverAvailable across configured surfaces. The cycle continues.

Ambiguous outputs are flagged for review, not activated automatically.

Operating detail

Roles, workflow, and human override.

Clear ownership at every step, and a parser that never overrides human judgment.

Governance roles
Owner
Approves and activates. Typically CIO or IC chair.
Reviewer
PM or research lead validates each tilt against the report.
Compliance
Verifies auditability; runs configured exports for committee or regulatory use.
Ops / Admin
Controls publishing scope and rollback, where configured.
Known limits & human override
•Narrative compression can miss nuance; every extraction is surfaced for review.
•Conflicting statements require owner judgment, not parser arbitration.
•Parser suggestions remain draft-level until the owner approves.
•Any activation can be superseded by a newer approved version.
•Where configured, rollback to a prior approved version is supported and logged.
